From the author · 作者寄语

Ideas should have somewhere to keep moving.

Hello, I am Molin Wu, the maintainer of TIAE. This is an open community for world-model research. We will share new TIAE results and ideas, and welcome reproductions, constructive criticism, collaboration, and early-stage discussion.

你好，我是 TIAE 第一作者孙俊涵。这里是一个面向世界模型研究的开放交流社区。我们会持续同步 TIAE 的实验进展与新想法，也欢迎复现、建设性批评、合作和自由讨论。

TIAE's launch builds upon the original TIAE work; thanks to Yann LeCun and members of the JEPA community. This space keeps that conversation open: research questions first, status second.
